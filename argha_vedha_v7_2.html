Pre-registered forecasts — five arms, one sky

Each forecast below was written down on July 3, 2026 — before the outcome exists. Nobody may adjust their claim once the window opens. In October–December we compare every arm against what gold and silver actually did, using one rule for everyone: did prices in the claimed window beat normal drift? Winners and losers are recorded publicly in the rulebook. That is what makes this science rather than storytelling.

PR-001 · The Empirical Model this app, rulebook v5
A computer model built from 58 years of daily prices and only the planetary rules that survived testing in two separate eras.

Expects: a solid bullish stretch Aug 31 → Sep 16, staying constructive to mid-Oct. Then a genuine weak spell Oct 18 → Nov 16 — the Sun (gold's main planet) passes through Libra, its weakest sign. The strongest window of all comes late: Dec 16 → Jan 10, when Jupiter, freshly in Leo, turns retrograde.
In plain words: patience — the model likes September, dislikes late October, loves the year-end.
PR-002 · The Classical Reading Kishore
The traditional interpretation: Jupiter fully matured in Cancer, its sign of exaltation, is the great wealth-expander.

Expects: gold's strongest run Aug 10 → Oct 10. Any dips along the way are consolidation — the market catching its breath — not a real decline.
In plain words: the rally starts earlier and runs on exaltation itself; weakness is temporary discipline, not danger.
PR-003 · The Public Panel four published astrologers
AstroAnanta (Nov 2025) — historic highs for gold through the whole Cancer transit, June to October. Bullish.
Astrological Musings (Jan 2026) — the contrarian school: gold's shine fades after Jupiter enters exaltation, just as it did in 2015. Bearish.
AstroIsha (Jan 2026) — get out of gold trading before November. Cautious.
Aghortantra (Mar 2026) — gold "out of reach of the common man" after June. Bullish, but this source sells consultations, so weigh accordingly.
How to read the verdict when it comes:
Strong gold from mid-August → the classical reading and the bulls win. Strength that waits until September → the empirical model wins. Fading through the whole transit → the contrarian school was right, and the popular exaltation rule takes real damage. A late-October slump inside a larger rise → PR-001's most distinctive call comes true.
PR-001 · The Empirical Model this app, rulebook v5
Silver moves with the Moon, so its windows are short and rhythmic rather than one long wave.

Expects: avoid late July through August (Jul 24–28, Aug 5–13, Aug 15–30 — waning Moons plus the eclipse). Then a chain of short bullish bursts: Sep 11–15, Oct 3–6, Oct 28–Nov 2, building to the two best windows of the half-year: Nov 22–29 and Dec 18–23. One more caution zone: Nov 6–11 and early January.
In plain words: August is hostile, autumn improves in pulses, and late November through late December is silver's real season.

Expects: Moon-driven volatility throughout, with Venus lending support whenever it sits in favorable signs — swings rather than a single trend, riding gold's exaltation-era strength.
PR-003 · The Public Panel published astrologers
AstroIsha (Jan 2026) — the most specific silver call: prices rise June through November 2026 while Jupiter transits the Moon's own sign. Bullish.
AstroAnanta (Nov 2025) — silver benefits doubly through the transit: as stored wealth and as an industrial metal. Bullish.

Silver strong in August → the model's hostile-August call fails early. A quiet August followed by November–December strength → the model's rhythm reading wins. Steady rise June through November → AstroIsha takes the crown. Weakness throughout → the contrarian school again.
